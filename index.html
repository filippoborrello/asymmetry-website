Our mission

Asymmetry highlights student research, faculty perspectives, and commentary on current economic issues.

Our mission is to make economic ideas and literature more accessible to a broader audience, while giving students hands-on experience in research, writing, editing, and publication.

What we publish

Student research

Original analysis by Georgetown undergraduates, from data-driven studies to reviews of published papers.

Faculty perspectives

Insight from Georgetown economists on their work and on the questions shaping the field.

Commentary

Clear, readable takes on current economic issues, from trade and housing to the national debt.

Write for Asymmetry

Analysis

An original, data-driven piece in the style of an academic journal article, with visualizations and, ideally, faculty guidance.

Opinion

Any topic you care about, with room for creative structure. Trade wars, housing, a policy debate.

Research Review

Walk readers through one published paper, then give your own reflection and its policy implications.

Length

Long form runs 1,000–1,500 words (2,000 max) in the spring issue. Short takes are up to 500 words and run when ready.

Have an idea? Write it.

No experience needed. Send two or three topics, each with a research question and a few sources, and we’ll pair you with peer editors.
