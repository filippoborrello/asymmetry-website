About Asymmetry

Our mission

Asymmetry highlights student research, faculty perspectives, and commentary on current economic issues.

What we do

Our mission is to make economic ideas and literature more accessible to a broader audience, while giving students hands-on experience in research, writing, editing, and publication.

Why we started

Getting involved in economics research as an undergraduate is hard. The barrier to entry is high, and building relationships with faculty takes time. At the same time, the kind of writing you find in The Economist or the Financial Times, which applies economics to real-world topics, is largely missing on campus.

Asymmetry is a place for that writing. Inspired by Equilibrium at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, we publish an annual spring issue of student work, plus short takes throughout the year.

What students gain

Research

Develop a question, review the literature, and work with data and sources.

Writing

Turn complex ideas into clear, readable pieces for a broad audience.

Editing

Give and receive peer review, with faculty review for long-form pieces.

Publication

See your work published in Georgetown’s undergraduate economics journal.
